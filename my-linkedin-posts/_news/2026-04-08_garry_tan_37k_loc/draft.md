This CEO writes 10,000 lines of code per day, part-time!
But his code and his website are full of shit.
Garry Tan is the CEO of Y Combinator, one of the most important startup accelerators in the world.
Garry is amazing. He's probably a good CEO and a great startup advisor. I love his advice on building startups and finding the right niche for your idea.
But Garry is not a software engineer, so we need to take his advice with a grain of salt.
A mountain of salt, to be clear.

For a while now, Garry has advertised his capability of writing a lot of lines of code per day or per week. He advertises that he can write 10,000 lines of code per day across multiple projects in parallel.

He even created a specific framework for writing code using Claude Code. He calls it G-Stack.
